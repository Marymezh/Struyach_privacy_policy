How to Delete Your Struyach Athlete Account

Struyach Fitness Apps

You can permanently delete your Struyach Athlete account and all associated data directly from within the app, at any time, without needing to contact support.

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Open the Struyach Athlete app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down to the "Log out or delete account" section.
  4. Tap "Delete account and all data".
  5. Read the confirmation warning and type "Confirm" in the text field to proceed.
  6. Tap Delete. Your account and all associated data will be permanently removed immediately.
This action is irreversible. Once confirmed, your account cannot be recovered.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account profile (name, email, avatar photo)
  • Your personal records (weightlifting and gymnastics)
  • All photos and videos you uploaded to comments
  • All comments you posted, across all training plans and blog posts
  • Your liked posts, liked workouts, and completed-workout progress
  • Your workout diary entries
  • Your notification preferences and device token

What is not affected

Comments and content posted by other users that reference or reply to you are not deleted, since they belong to those other users' own accounts. Deleting your account only removes data tied to your own account.

Timeline

Deletion is immediate — there is no waiting period. Once you confirm the deletion, your data is removed from our systems right away.

Need help?

If you're unable to access the app to delete your account yourself (for example, if you've lost access to your device), contact us at feedback.struyach@gmail.com and we will process the deletion request on your behalf.
